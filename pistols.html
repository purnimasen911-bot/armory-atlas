Class 01 · Handguns

Pistols & Revolvers

Short firearms designed to be fired from one hand. The class splits into two main branches: semi-automatic pistols, which feed cartridges from a magazine, and revolvers, which hold rounds in a rotating cylinder. Below are five handguns notable for their design influence or sheer numbers.

Semi-automatic · Striker-fired

Glock 17

Austria1982Gaston Glock

The first commercially successful polymer-framed pistol. Its simplified "Safe Action" mechanism and corrosion-resistant frame reset expectations for weight, capacity, and reliability, and it became a default for police forces worldwide.

Why two action types?

Semi-automatics use the energy of each fired cartridge to eject the spent case and load the next from a magazine; capacity is high but the mechanism is more complex. Revolvers keep rounds in a cylinder that simply rotates to the next chamber — fewer moving parts, very forgiving of neglect, but lower capacity. Each trades off reliability, capacity, and simplicity differently.
