Class 03 · Smoothbore

Shotguns

Smoothbore long arms that fire a spread of small pellets ("shot") or a single heavy projectile ("slug") rather than a single rifled bullet. They are measured in gauge — the bore's diameter expressed by the number of lead balls of that bore weighing one pound. The most common sporting gauge is 12 gauge (~18.5 mm bore).

Gauge, demystified

A 12-gauge bore has a diameter such that twelve solid lead spheres of that diameter weigh one pound; smaller numbers mean larger bores. Thus 10-gauge is larger than 12-gauge, and 20-gauge smaller. The popular .410 bore is named by its actual 0.410-inch calibre, not by the gauge system.
