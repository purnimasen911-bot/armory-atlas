Before anything else — safety

This site describes firearms as objects of technical and historical interest. It does not sell, endorse, or instruct in the use of weapons. Handling any firearm carries serious risk and serious legal responsibility; start with the four universal safety rules and the law where you live.

Armory Atlas

Armory Atlas is an educational reference and does not sell firearms or ammunition, nor does it provide instruction in their manufacture or use. Firearm laws vary widely by country and region — always check and comply with the law where you live. In India, civilian possession of a firearm requires a licence under the Arms Act, 1959 and the Arms Rules, 2016.
